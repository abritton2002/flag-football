Meet Coach Idalys

Experience that connects with young athletes.

Idalys Acosta has been involved in flag football for nearly a decade, beginning as a player and continuing to compete today. Through Acosta Athletics & Training, she is using her experience to help young athletes develop the skills, confidence, and understanding they need to grow in the game.

Her goal is to give back to the sport by creating opportunities for athletes, especially girls, in a game that continues to grow every year.

Training Philosophy

Training With Purpose

Training should build more than athletic ability. Every session is designed to help athletes improve their technique, understand the game, compete with confidence, and learn how to carry themselves on and off the field.

01

Skill Development

Focused work on movement, catching, route running, defensive skills, and football fundamentals.

02

Game IQ

Helping athletes understand spacing, timing, decision-making, and how to read the field.

03

Confidence

Building athletes who trust their preparation and compete with energy.

04

Opportunity

Supporting the growth of flag football and helping more girls get involved in the sport.

Training Options

Choose the right training format

Focused individual coaching and competitive group sessions give athletes room to grow at the pace and intensity that fits them.

1-on-1 Training

Personalized training designed around the athlete's current skill level, goals, and position needs. Ideal for athletes who want focused coaching, faster feedback, and individualized development.

  • 1-hour session
  • Individualized coaching
  • Skill-specific drills
  • Position-focused development
Book 1-on-1 Training

Group Training

Small-group sessions that help athletes develop skills in a competitive, high-energy environment. Great for athletes who want reps, competition, and team-style training.

  • 1.5-hour session
  • Limited spots available
  • Competitive group environment
  • Skill development
  • Game-like reps
